Quiet TV original weather materials

cumulus-material.rgba, stratus-material.rgba, cumulus-volume.rgba and moon-albedo.rgba are original
mathematical data generated by scripts/generate_weather_materials.py.
cloud-impostors.rgba is packed from original AI-assisted cloud artwork in
scripts/weather-materials/cloud-source.png by scripts/generate_weather_impostors.py.
cloud-blanket.rg8 packs the original blanket density and overhead ambient
transmission as two-component RG8 data. It is generated by the same script.
The black-background source is encoded as continuous coverage. Relief normals
and diffuse visibility are weighted by coverage for stable filtering; they are
artistic lighting proxies, not measured cloud geometry. Packing and hashes are
in weather-gpu/impostors.json.
NumPy and Pillow are used only for offline authoring; APK builds use checked-in data. Their
parameters, packing and hashes are in weather-gpu/materials.json. They contain
no Apple Weather resources or third-party photographs. The moon albedo is an
artistic crater/maria pattern, not a geographically accurate surface map.
